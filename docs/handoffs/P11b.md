**F-INV-REQ** (`requisitions/service.py`, `purchase.py`, `views.py`, `urls.py`):

| Step | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Request | `InternalRequisition` (number `SOL-`), lines in base UoM (presentation factor from P09), delivery warehouse = requesting warehouse; DRAFT edit/delete only. |
| Approval | `:submit` → P07 `WorkflowService.request_approval`; `:approve`/`:reject` (reason required) with SoD (requester never decides, `sod_violation`) and `WorkflowService.decide`. |
| Sourcing (D-012) | Runs automatically at approval as a **system-policy** action (`SourcingDecision.created_by_kind`, `policy_ref` D-012, audited) and on demand via `:source?mode=simulate|apply`. Candidates: other warehouses of the same company with free stock, ordered by the company priority list, then highest available, then code. Each source warehouse gets one P09 `Inventory.create_transfer` (DRAFT/SUBMITTED transfer **with reservations**); the remainder becomes `TO_PROCURE`. Invariant `qty_transfer + qty_procure + qty_cancelled ≤ base_qty` in service and DB check. |
| Concurrency | P09 reserves under row locks and refuses more than the free quantity; a sourcing that lost the race (`stock_insufficient`) rolls back to its savepoint, re-reads availability and sends the rest to procurement (no error, no over-allocation). |
| Consolidation (D-031) | `POST consolidations:run?dry_run=true|false` groups TO_PROCURE remainders by item + presentation + needed-by week (Monday) + delivery warehouse; lineage of every source line/qty; Σ sources = consolidated qty (checked). Dry run writes nothing. |
| Procurement (D-011) | Per consolidated line: `purchasing.interfaces.valid_vendor_price` (approved award ≤ 90 days) and line total (COP) ≤ company limit (default COP 5,000,000, `RequisitionSettings.auto_po_limit`) → one DRAFT PO per vendor through `purchasing.interfaces.draft_order` (it enters the normal PO approval; nothing is sent); otherwise a `BuyerTask` (`auto_po_limit_exceeded` warning, or `vendor_price_missing`). |
| Fulfilment | `sync_fulfilment`: received P09 transfers fulfil their lineage quantities; received PO quantities (P11a `received_qty`) are split over the consolidated sources in needed-by order; lines → FULFILLED, requisition → FULFILLED. `:cancel` (reason) cancels the unsourced remainder; with fulfilled quantities it closes as PARTIALLY_FULFILLED_CLOSED. |
| Purchase requisitions | `/requisitions` CRUD + `:submit/:approve/:reject` (SoD) + `:to-rfq` (P11a `rfq_from_requisition`, lines keep `source_line_id`) and `:to-po` (`draft_order`). |

**F-PORTAL-SUP** (`portal/service.py`, `views.py`, `urls.py`, `bridge.py`, `crypto.py`): every route of `portal.yaml ›
/api/portal/v1/supplier/*` (profile, change requests, documents, rfqs, quotes, purchase-orders + `:acknowledge`,
shipments, invoices, payments) plus `/kpis`. The supplier = purchasing supplier linked to the token's party in the token's
company; reads filter by it in the repository layer; foreign ids → 404; scope ids in query/body → 403
`external_account_scope_violation`; allowlisted response fields only. Quotes go through P11a `portal_submit_quote`
(channel `supplier_portal`) and are refused after the deadline (`portal_rfq_closed`); acknowledgement through
`portal_acknowledge_order` (or a change request: order stays SENT, shown `CHANGE_REQUESTED`). Shipment notices
(`AE-`, qty ≤ open qty) are visible to receiving (`notices_for_receiving`, `purchasing.shipment_notice.read`).
Profile changes: one pending at a time (`portal_profile_change_pending`); bank account encrypted at rest (only last 4 in
clear), **two different internal approvers** (`purchasing.supplier_profile_change.approve`, SoD) before
`SupplierMasterData` changes; other fields need one approval. Invoice uploads are DRAFT items (SHA-256 + size, ≤ 2 MB XML),
never posted. Payments = own AP open items with P16 `PaymentStatus` vouchers. KPIs `sp_rfq_abiertas`,
`sp_oc_por_confirmar`, `sp_facturas_por_pagar`, `sp_pagos_periodo` (+ previous period) in the shell's `KpiValue` shape.

## Decisions taken under ambiguity

1. **Automatic sourcing at approval** (F-INV-REQ "system action under policy after approval"); `:source` re-runs it on
   demand. Approvers do not need inventory permissions: the system-policy action is logged with policy D-012.
2. **Line status** after sourcing: `TO_PROCURE` while any procurement remainder is not consolidated yet (also for mixed
   lines), else `RESERVED`; `IN_PURCHASE` once the PO draft covers it; `FULFILLED` when fulfilled = requested − cancelled.
3. **Contract status mapping**: `status` keeps the contract enum (IN_PROGRESS→APPROVED, FULFILLED and
   PARTIALLY_FULFILLED_CLOSED→POSTED); the full state is `requisition_status` (CCR P11b-2).
4. **D-011 limit** compares the consolidated line total in COP (award unit price × FX × qty) with the company limit
   (default 5 000 000); one DRAFT PO per vendor per consolidation run.
5. **`consolidations:run`** executes synchronously and answers the contract `Operation` (202, SUCCEEDED) with the result;
   default `dry_run=true` (safer).
6. **Bank-change SoD**: two approvals from two different internal principals; any rejection ends the change; one pending
   change per supplier (partial unique index).
7. **Portal PO "changes requested"**: recorded as a `PoAcknowledgement(accepted=false)`; the order stays SENT (P11a has no
   CHANGE_REQUESTED state).
8. **Payments**: own AP open items + P16 `PaymentStatus` vouchers (fake in tests); `sp_pagos_periodo` sums POSTED vouchers
   with `posted_on` in the selected month.

## How to use / demo

```text
POST /api/v1/purchasing/internal-requisitions {document_date, requesting_warehouse_id, needed_by, lines[{line_no, item_id, quantity{value,uom}}]}
POST …/internal-requisitions/{id}:submit   → (another user) :approve {reason}   → sourcing result in "sourcing"
POST …/internal-requisitions/{id}:source?mode=simulate|apply
POST /api/v1/purchasing/consolidations:run?dry_run=true|false   GET /api/v1/purchasing/consolidations/{id}
Portal (erp-portal token of a supplier account): GET /api/portal/v1/supplier/rfqs · POST …/rfqs/{id}/quotes
  · POST …/purchase-orders/{id}:acknowledge · POST …/shipments · POST …/profile/change-requests · GET …/kpis?period=2026-11
```
Setup: `purchasing.requisitions.service.configure(uow, company, auto_po_limit=…, warehouse_priority=[…])`.
