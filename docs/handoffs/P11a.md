## How to use it

```text
POST /api/v1/purchasing/rfqs                       {document_date, deadline?, invited_vendors[], lines[{line_no, item_id, quantity{value,uom}}]}
POST /api/v1/purchasing/rfqs/{id}:send
POST /api/v1/purchasing/rfqs/{id}/quotes           {party{id}, currency, fx_rate?, lead_time_days, payment_terms_days, freight_amount?, lines[{line_no, quantity{value,uom}, factor_to_base?, unit_price{amount}, discount_pct?, tax_code?, tax_recoverable?, freight_amount?}]}
POST /api/v1/purchasing/rfqs/{id}:close | :evaluate | :reopen {reason, parameters{deadline}}
GET  /api/v1/purchasing/rfqs/{id}/comparison
POST /api/v1/purchasing/rfqs/{id}:award            {reason?, parameters{quote_id | lines[{rfq_line_id, quote_id}]}}
POST /api/v1/purchasing/rfqs/{id}:approve-award    {reason, parameters{approve: true|false}}   → DRAFT PO(s)
POST /api/v1/purchasing/orders/{id}:submit | :approve | :send
POST /api/v1/purchasing/receipts                   {order{id}, warehouse_id, lines[{order_line_id, quantity}]}  then :simulate / :validate
POST /api/v1/purchasing/vendor-invoices            {party{id}, vendor_invoice_number, order{id}, lines[{order_line_id, quantity, unit_price, tax_code, withholding_codes[]}]}  then :match / :simulate / :post
GET  /api/v1/purchasing/ap/open-items | ap/aging?as_of= | reports/grni?as_of=
```

Every write needs `Idempotency-Key`; with several companies send `X-Company-Id`. Company setup (seed):
`purchasing.services.common.configure(uow, company, scoring_weights=…, receipt_tolerance_pct=…,
invoice_qty_tolerance_pct=…, invoice_price_tolerance_pct=…, po_auto_approve_limit=…)` and suppliers via
`purchasing.services.suppliers.create_supplier`.

## Decisions taken under ambiguity

1. **Supplier master** lives in purchasing (`purchasing_supplier`, linked to the P10 party by `party_id`); the
   contract has no supplier route, so it is created by service/seed (CCR P11a-6 §4). Vendor rating 0–5 (default 3).
2. **Comparison basis**: every quote is compared on the RFQ's requested base quantity (unit landed COP × requested
   qty), so partial offers are comparable; quotes not covering all lines are excluded (`INCOMPLETE`). Lead time of a
   quote = max(header, line lead times). Lead-time factor `(min+1)/(lead+1)` (avoids division by zero); payment terms
   `terms/max`; rating `rating/5`; points rounded to 4 decimals half-up.
3. **Tie rule reporting**: the explanation states the rule that broke a tie at the top (lower landed COP total →
   earlier receipt → vendor code).
4. **`:close` before the deadline** is refused (`invalid_state_transition`); the deadline closes the RFQ (job
   `close_due_rfqs`). Reopening requires a reason and a new future deadline; LATE quotes become RECEIVED.
5. **Awarding an excluded (LATE) quote** is refused with `invalid_state_transition` (`quote_late` is a warning code).
6. **Tolerances default to 0 %** (most restrictive): any over-receipt or price/qty difference needs a breach approval
   until the company configures tolerances. PO auto-approve limit default 0 (every PO needs approval).
7. **Price differences** on invoices go to `PURCHASE_PRICE_VARIANCE` (no `StockService.revalue` yet — CCR P11a-5).
8. **Receipt GL** is posted by purchasing (Dr INVENTORY / Cr GRNI) — CCR P11a-5 asks P09 to not post a second entry.
9. **Rates**: quotes use the rate given with the quote or the ledger rate on/before the quote date
   (`quote_currency_rate_missing` otherwise); receipts use the ledger rate on the receipt date, else the PO rate;
   all GL lines are posted in COP (foreign-currency AP revaluation S12 is not in this part).
10. **Quote attachment download** is a service (no undocumented route, UR-22) until CCR P11a-3 adds the route.
11. **Status in contract terms**: receipts expose `status: POSTED` + `receipt_status: VALIDATED`; quotes keep
    `quote_status`; RFQ/PO use the FEATURES-ADDENDUM state names (CCR P11a-1).

## Known limitations (P11 scope not delivered by P11a)

Not implemented (routes exist only in the frozen contract, no fake endpoints were added): vendor credit notes,
documento soporte + adjustment notes (R-FE-08, S9), landed costs (S3), received e-invoices ingestion + RADIAN events
(R-FE-10), payment proposals / payments / offsets (T-P11-08/10/12), quote CSV import (`quotes:import`), fixed-asset
capitalization from invoice lines (P14), the `GET /api/v1/kpis/compras` KPI provider and the compras dashboard
widgets, the "Facturas de proveedor" and "Proveedores" pages. The original prompt's T-P11-nn tests are not mandatory
for P11a (its mandatory tests are T-PUR-CYCLE-01…06). DEV approvals need the P21 seed policies (CCR P11a-6).
