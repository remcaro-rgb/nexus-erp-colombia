## Decisions (non-obvious)
* **Portal excluded** (P10b owns `backend/sales/portal/**`): T-SAL-PRICING-04 is proven on the frozen projections in
  `sales/interfaces.py`, which P10b must use. The customer sees only the contract `Customer*` fields and, for prices,
  `engine.explain()` (final unit price + currency) — never cost, snapshot, formula id/code/text or trace.
* Internal users with the confidential grant on `sales.price_snapshot` see the `cost` input and the explain trace (which
  contains the evaluated expression steps) — FEATURES-ADDENDUM F-SAL-PRICING › Security restricts cost to cost roles and
  formulas/cost to non-portal users; without the grant both are stripped.
* Default discount limit is 0 % when no `sales_settings` row exists (fail safe: every manual discount is approved).
* Formula money inputs are COP; the result is converted with the document FX and rounded to the currency minor unit
  (`kernel.money.minor_units`: COP = 2).
* Detailed document states are exposed as `document_status`; `status` keeps the generic contract enum (CCR P10a-1).
* A credit note above the open balance of the invoice is refused (`treasury_overapplication`); customer advances are not
  in P10a.
* Kit components are not reserved at order confirmation (the BOM is exploded at delivery/invoice); a short component
  fails the whole delivery (nothing issued, nothing posted).
* Fiscal hand-off happens only through the outbox event; P10a never calls DIAN or a simulator directly.
* CRM has no registry module of its own: its board is mounted under `ventas › oportunidades`.
* No new permission codes were needed (all `sales.*`, `crm.*` and `portal.customer_*` codes already exist).
