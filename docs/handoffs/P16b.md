## What was built
* **Intercompany [P]** (`intercompany.py`, tables `consolidation_ic_*`):
  * Agreements per company pair (A sells to B): role overrides in `accounts` (defaults IC_RECEIVABLE, IC_PAYABLE,
    REVENUE_IC, COGS, INVENTORY, VAT_PAYABLE, VAT_RECOVERABLE, EXPENSE_IC, BANK), `pricing_rule`
    TRANSFER_PRICE|COST_PLUS (+ `markup_pct`, enforced on goods lines), `auto_mirror`, `default_warehouse_id`.
  * **S10**: `post_sale` posts A (Dr IC_RECEIVABLE 178,500 / Cr REVENUE_IC 150,000 / Cr VAT_PAYABLE 28,500;
    Dr COGS 115,000 / Cr INVENTORY 115,000) and `mirror_sales_invoice` posts B (Dr INVENTORY 150,000 /
    Dr VAT_RECOVERABLE 28,500 / Cr IC_PAYABLE 178,500) **in the same unit of work**, linked by `ic_link_id`
    (`IntercompanyLink`: A document + entries, B document + entries, sale value, A's cost, goods value).
    B's side is written by the **IC system principal** (`SYSTEM_PRINCIPAL`, `principal_type=service`,
    `on_behalf_of` = the user): recorded in the B document, B's journal entry (`created_by`), the audit entry and
    any event actor. The user needs `intercompany.document.post` in A only; B's users see B's documents (RLS).
  * IC settlement (B pays A) and netting, both sides at once; buyer consumption (`record_consumption`).
  * IC reconciliation report: A's IC position vs B against B's vs A (party = company on the IC lines); MIRRORED /
    MISMATCH per agreement; only pairs whose two companies the caller may read are listed.
* **Consolidation [A]** (flag `consolidation`, default off; `service.py`, pure `engine.py`):
  * Groups (first company = parent), members (ownership %, FULL / PROPORTIONAL / EQUITY, historical equity rate,
    parent's investment account), chart mapping to the group CoA (longest prefix; identity by default).
  * Runs DRAFT → `:preview` (SimulationResult, nothing persisted) → `:post` (SoD: preparer ≠ poster; one posted run
    per group+period; `closed_through` blocks posting with `period_closed`) → `reverse_run` (reversal entries).
  * Translation: assets/liabilities at closing, P&L at average, equity at historical (rates from the parent's
    `ledger.ExchangeRate`, COP per unit; missing → `fx_rate_missing`); CTA to OCI (`381595`, synthetic).
  * Eliminations in the **consolidation book** (`consolidation_entry[_line]`, append-only DB trigger; subsidiaries'
    books never touched): IC balances (mismatch → `199995` + `intercompany.mismatch.detected.v1`), IC revenue vs
    COGS, unrealized profit in the buyer's ending inventory (S10: 35,000 when unsold), investment vs equity
    (goodwill in functional currency at closing rate, residual to CTA), NCI share of equity, result and CTA,
    equity-method pickup, balanced manual adjustments (run lines: `quantity × unit_price`, + debit / − credit).
  * Reports (`ReportResult`, `synthetic: true`, JSON or `format=csv` with formula-injection hardening): consolidated
    balance (with balance check), income statement (net income, NCI, attributable to parent), eliminations.
* **API**: every operation of `consolidation.yaml` and `intercompany.yaml` (the latter mounted at
  `api/v1/intercompany/` from this app via `EXTRA_URL_MOUNTS`), `ModuleSecuredView`, deny by default, permission
  checked for **every company** a tenant-level resource touches, hidden company → 404, `Idempotency-Key` on every
  write (replays), `If-Match` on PATCH (428/412), DELETE archives masters / cancels draft runs (posted → 409).
* **Security/DB**: forced RLS on all 11 tables (pair predicate for agreements/links, company scope for IC
  documents, tenant scope + application check for groups/runs/book); append-only triggers on the book and IC
  documents; IC links immutable except `consumed_amount`.
* **UI** (`frontend/src/features/consolidation/`): page "Intercompañía y consolidación" contributed to
  `tesoreria › intercompania` through the shell contribution API (`module.ts`, id `consolidacion`, no registry
  module of its own; the P16a descriptor needs no change). Tabs (WAI-ARIA, arrow keys): Conciliación IC (CSV),
  Acuerdos (create), Grupos y corridas (create run, preview, post with confirmation dialog), Estados consolidados.

## Parameters/sources
No regulatory parameter is used. VAT amounts are supplied by the caller (tax engine); the 19 % in tests is the S10
scenario value. Group accounts (`381595`, `390505`, `599005`, `160505`, `429905`, `199995`, `424005`, `120505`) and
the test chart are SYNTHETIC — NOT LEGALLY VERIFIED. FX rates come from `ledger.ExchangeRate` (TRM feed is P16a).

## Known limitations
* The mirror posts B's GL directly; B's AP open item and the goods receipt (stock quantities) need P11/P09 hooks (CCR P16b-2).
* Ledgers are COP (D-001): translation applies when the presentation currency differs from COP; a member with a
  non-COP functional currency is not modelled.
* Investment elimination uses the subsidiary's current book equity (no acquisition-date / post-acquisition reserve
  split); goodwill impairment is not computed. Equity-method associates' unrealized profit is not eliminated.
* Members/ownership/mapping/reversal/close are service-level until CCR P16b-1 adds them to the contract; the UI
  shows groups/runs created through the API (no member editor).
* Unrealized profit relies on `record_consumption` from inventory (until then, all IC goods count as unsold).
* Report export formats: json and csv (xlsx/pdf are the reporting module's, P17).

## Decisions taken under ambiguity
1. `intercompany.yaml` has no owner in the cloud plan; P16b (the IC part of P16) implements it from its own app
   (`EXTRA_URL_MOUNTS`) without editing the generated contract.
2. The reconciliation report returns 200 with MISMATCH rows (the contract's `intercompany_mismatch` 422 would hide
   the data the report exists to show); mismatches are also warnings and an event at consolidation posting.
3. SoD on `:post` (preparer ≠ poster, D-023, most restrictive); one posted run per group and period.
4. Groups are visible only when **all** member companies are in the context and authorized; agreements when either
   company is (writes need both).
5. IC lines carry `party = (company id, kind "company")` to pair positions without a party master entry.
6. DRF's `?format=` URL override is disabled for this module so the contract's `format` parameter selects the export.
