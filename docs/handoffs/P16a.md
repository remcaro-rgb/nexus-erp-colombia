## How to use it

```text
POST /api/v1/treasury/bank-accounts   {code, name, bank_code, account_number, currency, account_id(ledger), fund_id?}  (creates the BANK fund when fund_id is absent)
POST /api/v1/treasury/cash-boxes      {code, name, currency, account_id, fund_id?}
POST /api/v1/treasury/concepts        {code, name, direction IN|OUT|BOTH, account_role, requires_dimension?}
POST /api/v1/treasury/receipts        {document_date, fund_id, payment_method, party{id,kind}?, lines[...]}
     line kinds: {line_no, open_item_id, applied_amount}            → application to an AR (RC) / AP (CE) open item
                 {line_no, concept_id, applied_amount | unit_price+quantity, dimensions?} → concept
                 {line_no, tax_code, unit_price(=base)}              → withholding (amount from the tax engine)
                 {line_no, applied_amount}                           → customer / vendor advance
POST /api/v1/treasury/receipts/{id}:simulate | :submit | :approve | :reject {reason} | :post | :reverse {reason, effective_date?}
     (same for /disbursements)
POST /api/v1/treasury/payment-batches {document_date, payment_date, fund_id, lines[{line_no, party{id}, open_item_id?, applied_amount, tax_code?}]}
POST /api/v1/treasury/payment-batches/{id}:submit | :approve | :generate-file | :execute | :mark-sent
POST /api/v1/treasury/statements:import (multipart file) · POST /statement-lines/{id}:match {parameters{voucher_id}} | :unmatch
GET  /api/v1/treasury/funds/{id}/snapshot · GET /api/v1/kpis/tesoreria?period=YYYY-MM
```

Company setup (seed): `treasury.services.common.configure(uow, company, rc_prefix="RC-", ce_prefix="CE-",
rc_approval_threshold=None|amount, ce_approval_threshold=0|None|amount, dual_approval_threshold="20000000")`, the P07
policies `TES-RC` / `TES-CE` / `TES-DUAL` (quorum 2), and the ledger data of CCR P16a-3.

## Decisions taken under ambiguity

1. **Line kind derivation** from the frozen contract line (no `kind` property): open item → application; concept →
   concept; tax code alone → withholding with `unit_price` = base (amount always from the tax engine; manual
   withholding overrides are not offered); otherwise advance. `account_code` / `item_id` are refused (`field_forbidden`).
2. **States**: the contract's `SUBMITTED` is the addendum's PENDING_APPROVAL. Below the threshold `:submit` approves
   automatically and `:post` works straight from DRAFT; at/above it `:post` answers `treasury_approval_required`.
   DELETE marks a draft `CANCELLED` (kept for the audit trail).
3. **Thresholds** (COP, `TreasurySettings`): RC none by default; CE `0` by default (every CE needs one approver — the
   addendum's "CE always above COP 0 when policy says so"); dual approval at COP 20,000,000 (addendum default) for CE
   and payment batches. Concept `approval_threshold` also triggers approval.
4. **Reversal** creates a numbered reversal voucher of the same type (`reversal_of` → original; D-017 "voiding is a
   reversal document") posted with the ledger's mirror entry on `effective_date` or today; the original keeps its
   number and only changes to REVERSED.
5. **Applications only in COP** vouchers of the item currency (CCR P16a-2); concept vouchers may use another currency
   at the ledger rate of the posting date.
6. **Pure concept vouchers** (no party): the posted event's required `party` is the company itself (`kind: other`).
7. **Batch execution** posts each CE immediately with the batch's approval (approvers copied to every CE); one CE per
   payee in order of first appearance; withholding on a batch line uses the line amount as base.
8. **Insufficient funds**: blocking (`422 treasury_insufficient_funds_warning`) when the fund forbids overdraft
   (default), otherwise a warning on the voucher and in `:simulate`.
9. **Bank statement import** generic CSV mapping is synthetic (per-bank formats pending EG-5); `:create-entry`,
   reconciliation rules and auto-match rules are not in this part.
