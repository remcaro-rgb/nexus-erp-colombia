# CCR P11a-5 — StockService for purchasing receipts and price differences (P09)

* From: P11a · To: P09a · Type: interface clarification · Status: OPEN

1. **Who posts the receipt GL.** P11a calls `StockService.receive(uow, company, DocumentRef("purchasing.receipt", id),
   lines, date)` with `unit_cost` = PO price in COP at the receipt-date rate and posts **itself** Dr INVENTORY /
   Cr GRNI (purpose `receipt`) in the same unit of work. For sources of type `purchasing.*` the inventory module must
   therefore return `journal_entry_id = None` (no second inventory entry), or the interface needs a contra-role
   parameter — please confirm which.
2. **Price difference on invoice** (shared/07 §2 "revalue remaining layers by Δ×qty_on_hand; remainder to COGS"):
   `kernel.interfaces.stock` has no `revalue`. Until it exists P11a books the whole difference to
   `PURCHASE_PRICE_VARIANCE` (documented in the handoff). Requested: `StockService.revalue(uow, company, source,
   item_id, warehouse_id, original_receipt: DocumentRef, delta_value_cop) -> StockMoveResult` returning the on-hand
   and consumed portions so P11a posts Dr/Cr INVENTORY + COGS.
3. Quality hold `[A]` at receipt is not used by P11a yet (`Inventory.quality_hold` exists).
