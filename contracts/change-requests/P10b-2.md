# CCR P10b-2 — additive routes and fields of the customer portal contract (`portal.yaml`, `sales.yaml`)

* From: P10b · To: contracts owner (integrator / P02 sources `contracts/src/modules/{portal,sales}.yaml`) · Type: additive · Status: OPEN
* Why: F-PORTAL-CUS needs a customer acceptance of quotes, a simulated payment-provider result (D-021), the open
  invoices to choose from, the dashboard KPIs and the back office of the portal; the frozen `portal.yaml` has none of
  them, and `CustomerRequestInput` (`additionalProperties: false`) cannot carry the items of a quote request.

## Portal routes (`/api/portal/v1/customer/*`, `x-portal: customer`, owner P10)
| Route | Permission | Body / answer |
|---|---|---|
| `POST quotes/{id}:accept` | `portal.customer_quote.update` (appended to the catalog) | ActionRequest → ActionResult `{status: ACCEPTED, resource: {type: sales.order, id, number}, data.quote}`; only SENT and not expired (`valid_until`), else 409 `invalid_state_transition`; drafts / other parties → 404 |
| `POST payment-intents/{id}:simulate` | `portal.customer_payment_intent.create` | `parameters.outcome ∈ {success, failure}` (DEV simulated provider, FIXTURE_NOT_CERTIFIED) → `CustomerPaymentIntent`; only CREATED, else 409 |
| `GET open-items` | `portal.customer_invoice.read` | page of `{document{type,id,number}, due_date, balance: Money}` (P10a `portal_open_items`) |
| `GET credit-notes/{id}/pdf`, `GET credit-notes/{id}/xml` | `portal.customer_credit_note.read` | as the invoice PDF/XML (labelled `SINTÉTICO — sin validez fiscal`) |
| `GET kpis?period=YYYY-MM` | `portal.customer_profile.read` | `{as_of, period, kpis[KpiValue], generated_at, portal}` (same pattern as CCR P13a-2 / P11b-2); the P08 shell calls `/api/portal/v1/kpis?portal=customer`, which nobody serves yet |

## Portal fields
* `CustomerRequest` + `CustomerRequestInput`: `number` (ro), `items` (`[{item_id uuid, quantity decimal-string, uom?}]`,
  QUOTE only), `changes` (object of `email|einvoice_email|phone|address|municipality_code|trade_name`, PROFILE_CHANGE
  only), `resolution` (ro), `quote_id`, `order_id` (ro). `request_type ∈ {QUOTE, PROFILE_CHANGE, GENERAL}`;
  `request_status ∈ {SUBMITTED, QUOTED, ORDERED, APPROVED, REJECTED, RESOLVED}`.
* `CustomerPaymentIntent.intent_status` keeps the contract enum (`CREATED`, `SUCCEEDED_SIMULATED`, `FAILED`,
  `CONFIRMED_BY_TREASURY`); FEATURES-ADDENDUM's `SIMULATED_SUCCEEDED`/`SIMULATED_FAILED`/`EXPIRED` are mapped to the
  first two contract values (EXPIRED not implemented).
* Statement `ReportResult.totals` adds `opening_balance`, `debit`, `credit`, `closing_balance`, `open_balance`,
  `aging` (bucket → COP); `format=csv|pdf` exports (both labelled).

## Internal routes (`/api/v1/sales/*`, `sales.yaml`)
| Route | Permission |
|---|---|
| `GET portal-requests`, `GET portal-requests/{id}` (filters `request_status`, `request_type`, `party`) | `sales.portal_request.read` |
| `POST portal-requests/{id}:quote` (`parameters{lines?, valid_until?, send?, currency?}` → DRAFT/SENT quote via P10a) | `sales.portal_request.update` (+ `sales.quote.create`/`update`) |
| `POST portal-requests/{id}:approve` (applies a PROFILE_CHANGE through `update_party`; closes a GENERAL request) | `sales.portal_request.approve` (+ `sales.party.update`) |
| `POST portal-requests/{id}:reject` (`reason` required → 422 `reason_required`) | `sales.portal_request.update` |
| `GET payment-intents`, `GET payment-intents/{id}` (with `receipt_id` of the DRAFT RC) | `sales.payment_intent.read` |

The four `sales.*` codes and `portal.customer_quote.update` are appended to `contracts/permissions/catalog.yaml`
(`source: P10b`). No new error codes (`payment_intent_amount_invalid`, `portal_document_not_owned`,
`external_account_scope_violation` exist).
