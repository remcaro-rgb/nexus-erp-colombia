# CCR P14-4 — sales invoice of an asset sale (P10)

* From: P14 · To: P10 (sales) · Type: interface · Status: OPEN
* P14 consumes `assets.interface.DisposalInvoicing.create_disposal_invoice(uow, DisposalInvoiceRequest) -> DocumentRef`
  (customer, date, description, net, VAT, VAT tax code, source = the disposal) **inside the disposal's unit of work**.
* Expected P10 behaviour: create the customer invoice with one **non-stock** line whose revenue account role is
  `ASSET_DISPOSAL_PROCEEDS`, compute VAT with the P06 engine, post its own entry `Dr AR / Cr VAT_PAYABLE /
  Cr ASSET_DISPOSAL_PROCEEDS (net)` and run the e-invoice flow as for any invoice. The disposal entry (per book) posts
  `Dr ACCUM_DEPRECIATION / Dr ACCUM_IMPAIRMENT / Dr ASSET_DISPOSAL_PROCEEDS (net) / Cr FIXED_ASSET_COST / Cr GAIN | Dr LOSS`,
  so the clearing account nets to zero and VAT/AR are posted once.
* Until P10 registers an implementation, a SALE disposal records the customer id; the clearing account shows the
  proceeds pending invoicing (tested in T-P14-06).
