# CCR P10b-1 — official part hooks for `sales.portal` in the sales app (migrations, models, portal mount)

* From: P10b · To: P10a (owner of `backend/sales/{apps.py,models.py,migrations/}`) · Type: wiring (no behaviour change) · Status: OPEN
* Why: P10b owns only `backend/sales/portal/**`. Its two tables (`sales_portal_request`, `sales_portal_payment_intent`,
  forced RLS) are models of the `sales` app label with migrations `0101_p10b_portal` / `0102_p10b_rls_guards` in
  `backend/sales/portal/migrations/`, and its portal routes are absolute (`api/portal/v1/customer/*`) while `sales.urls`
  appends them under `/api/v1/sales/`. Until official hooks exist, `sales/portal/extension.py` (same accepted pattern as
  P11b's `purchasing/requisitions/extension.py`) adds the part's migrations directory to `sales.migrations.__path__`
  (with a `sys.meta_path` finder that survives Django's reload of the package), imports `sales.portal.models`, and
  `sales/portal/urls.py` registers `EXTRA_URL_MOUNTS["api/portal/v1/customer/"]` on the sales `AppConfig` before
  `erp.discovery` reads it.
* Request (P10a or the integrator):
  1. `SalesConfig.EXTRA_URL_MOUNTS = {"api/portal/v1/customer/": ("sales.portal.urls:portal_patterns", "sales-portal")}`;
  2. `backend/sales/migrations/__init__.py`: append `backend/sales/portal/migrations` to `__path__` (inventory's hook);
  3. `backend/sales/models.py`: `from sales.portal.models import *  # noqa` at the end (guarded by `find_spec`).
  Then the bootstrap in P10b is a no-op (idempotent).
* Migration numbering: the part migrations are `01xx_p10b_*` and depend on `sales.0002_rls_guards`. A future P10a
  migration `0003_*` also depending on `0002` creates two leaf nodes; its author adds a merge migration (or depends on
  `0102_p10b_rls_guards`).
