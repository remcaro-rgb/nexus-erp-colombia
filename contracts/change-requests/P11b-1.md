# CCR P11b-1 — official extension hooks of the purchasing app for the P11b parts (P11a)

* From: P11b · To: P11a (owner of `backend/purchasing/**` except `requisitions/**`, `portal/**`) · Type: wiring · Status: OPEN
* Need: P11b owns only `backend/purchasing/requisitions/**` and `backend/purchasing/portal/**`, but its models, migrations
  and the absolute supplier-portal routes must belong to the `purchasing` app. P09a provided the equivalent hooks for
  P09b/P09c (`inventory/migrations/__init__.py` appends part directories to `__path__`; `inventory/models.py` imports the
  part models). The purchasing app has no such hook, and `purchasing/urls.py` mounts the part `urlpatterns` under
  `/api/v1/purchasing/`, where `/api/portal/v1/supplier/*` cannot live.
* Request (three small edits in P11a files):
  1. `backend/purchasing/migrations/__init__.py`: append `../requisitions/migrations` and `../portal/migrations` to
     `__path__` when the directories exist (copy of the inventory hook).
  2. `backend/purchasing/models.py`: import `purchasing.requisitions.models` and `purchasing.portal.models` when present.
  3. `PurchasingConfig.EXTRA_URL_MOUNTS = {"api/portal/v1/supplier/": ("purchasing.portal.urls:portal_patterns",
     "purchasing-portal")}` (`erp.discovery` reads it).
  Future P11a migrations: depend on `("purchasing", "0102_p11b_rls_guards")` (or add a merge migration) so the graph keeps
  one leaf.
* Meanwhile (P11b, `backend/purchasing/requisitions/extension.py`, `backend/purchasing/portal/urls.py`): importing the
  part packages (which `purchasing.urls` does whenever the URL configuration loads — `migrate`, `makemigrations --check`,
  `runserver`, every request process — and the part tests at collection) installs a `sys.meta_path` finder that adds
  the part migration directory to the search path of `purchasing.migrations` (it survives Django's reload of that
  package), imports the part models, and registers the portal mount on the app config before discovery reads
  `EXTRA_URL_MOUNTS`. All three are idempotent no-ops once the hooks above exist. Verified: `makemigrations --check`
  → "No changes detected"; `/api/portal/v1/supplier/*` resolves through the real `erp.urls`.
