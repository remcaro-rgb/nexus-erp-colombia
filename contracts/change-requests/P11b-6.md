# CCR P11b-6 — events and jobs around F-INV-REQ (P09, P07/P19 scheduler)

* From: P11b · To: P09 (inventory), P07 (jobs/consumers) · Type: integration · Status: OPEN
* Requests:
  1. P09 reorder job: create internal requisitions through `purchasing.requisitions.service.create(uow, company, data,
     source=DocumentRef("inventory.reorder", …))` (F-INV-REQ "supersedes").
  2. Fulfilment trigger: on `inventory.transfer.status_changed.v1` (to RECEIVED) and `purchasing.receipt.validated.v1`,
     call `purchasing.requisitions.service.sync_fulfilment(uow, requisition)` under a system principal of the tenant
     (outbox consumer). Today the function is called explicitly (tests, buyer action); no consumer is registered because
     the kernel has no system-principal context for consumers yet.
  3. Receiving UI: show `purchasing.portal.service.notices_for_receiving` (announced shipments of a PO) on the receipt
     screen (P09 shipment notice → receiving, FEATURES-ADDENDUM §20 stub).
