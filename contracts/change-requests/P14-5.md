* From: P14 · To: P05 · Type: integration · Status: OPEN
* `assets.reports.temporary_differences(entries, as_of) -> {asset_id: fiscal NBV − NIIF NBV}` gives the per-asset
  temporary difference from the asset register (S8: 426,666.64 after November 2026). The register report

* Request: when P05 builds the `TaxDifference` register (`/api/v1/ledger/tax-differences`), pull these values per
  period (source `assets.register`) instead of re-deriving them.
