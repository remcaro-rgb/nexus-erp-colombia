# CCR P16b-2 — sales/purchasing/inventory/treasury call the intercompany service (S10 integration)

* From: P16b · To: P10 (sales), P11 (purchasing), P09 (inventory), P16a (treasury) · Type: integration (no contract change) · Status: OPEN

`consolidation.interface.IntercompanyService` (registered in `kernel.services` by `consolidation.registry`) is the
frozen in-process contract. Every method runs **inside the caller's unit of work** (S10: a failure on either side
rolls back both companies).

1. **P10 sales**: when an invoice is posted to a customer that is another company of the tenant (an
   `IntercompanyAgreement` exists for `(seller, buyer)`), post A's entries with `party = PartyRef(company_b_id,
   "company")` on the IC_RECEIVABLE/REVENUE_IC/VAT_PAYABLE lines and call
   `services.get(IntercompanyService).mirror_sales_invoice(uow, IcSaleRequest(...))` in the same UoW
   (`a_entry_ids` = the posted entries). The caller needs `intercompany.document.post` in company A.
2. **P11 purchasing**: expose an in-UoW `register_ic_vendor_invoice(uow, ...)` so the mirror becomes a real AP
   document/open item of B (and creates the goods receipt with the StockService when the agreement has a
   `default_warehouse_id`). Until then the mirror posts the S10 GL directly in B
   (Dr INVENTORY or EXPENSE_IC / Dr VAT_RECOVERABLE / Cr IC_PAYABLE) and records an `IntercompanyDocument`.
3. **P09 inventory**: when B issues/sells goods received from an IC link, call
   `record_consumption(uow, link_id, value)` so consolidation stops eliminating that part of the unrealized profit.
4. **P16a treasury**: IC payments between group companies call `settle(uow, IcSettlementRequest(...))`; the KPI
   `saldos_intercompania` of `/api/v1/kpis/tesoreria` can be computed with
   `consolidation.intercompany.reconciliation_rows(ctx, as_of)` (Σ |difference|). The Tesorería descriptor needs no
   change: the "Intercompañía y consolidación" page is contributed by `frontend/src/features/consolidation/module.ts`.
