* From: P11a · To: P05 (`ledger/setup.py` ROLES), P21 (synthetic PUC determinations) · Type: data · Status: OPEN

Purchasing posts by role (shared/07 §2): `INVENTORY` (exists), `AP` (exists), and the new roles
`GRNI` (recepciones por facturar, control account reserved to `purchasing.` sources), `VAT_RECOVERABLE`,
`WHT_PAYABLE`, `PURCHASE_PRICE_VARIANCE`, `EXPENSE`. Please add them to `ledger.setup.ROLES` and map them in the
synthetic seed. `backend/purchasing/tests/test_real_ledger.py` shows a working synthetic mapping (account codes there
are SINTÉTICO, not a legal chart). `AP` and `GRNI` should be control accounts with `control_sources: ["purchasing."]`.
